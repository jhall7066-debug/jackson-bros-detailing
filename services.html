FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Do you come to me?

Yes. Jackson Bros is fully mobile. We detail your car at your home, apartment complex or workplace, as long as the property allows it and there's safe space to work.

What areas do you serve?

West Chester, Cincinnati and nearby towns including Liberty Township, Hamilton, Fairfield, Mason, Blue Ash and Middletown. Not sure you're in range? Call or text (513) 341-5374.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

Yes. We need access to an outdoor water spigot and a power outlet. If neither is available, we aren't able to do the detail.

How long does a detail take?

Most details take 2 to 5 hours depending on the package and the car's condition. Maintenance is about 1.5 hours, Basic about 2 and Premium about 3.

How much does a detail cost?

Packages start at $100 for Maintenance, $150 for Basic and $200 for Premium. Add-ons run $10 to $75. Extra charges apply for excessive dirt, pet hair, trash, stains or strong odors, and you'll be told before anything is added.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as the car is accessible, keys are provided if needed, and payment is made once the service is complete.

How do I pay?

Payment is due right after the detail is finished. We take cash, Cash App and Apple Pay. We don't accept checks or cards right now.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before your appointment. Later cancellations and no-shows are subject to a fee.
